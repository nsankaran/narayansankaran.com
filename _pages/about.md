I am an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Neuroscience](https://www.usfca.edu/arts-sciences/programs/undergraduate/neuroscience) at the [University of San Francisco](https://www.usfca.edu/), where I head the [Auditory Neuroscience & Perception Lab](https://narayansankaran.com/sankaran-lab/).

My research seeks a mechanistic account of how the human brain enables _music perception_ - a hallmark of human culture. Such insight informs our overall understanding of brain function and has the potential to unlock music-based interventions for treating mood, anxiety, and various neurological disorders. To do this work, I take an interdisciplinary approach, combining human neurophysiological measurements of brain activity (EEG, MEG, ECoG) with machine-learning, computational modelling, psychophysics and behavior.

In addition to my scientific research program, I write about the ethical and societal implications of emerging neurotechnologies like brain computer interfaces.

Before joining the faculty at USF, I completed two postdoctoral fellowships: one in the [lab of Edward Chang](https://changlab.ucsf.edu/) (Department of Neurosurgery, UCSF) and another at the [Kavli Center for Ethics, Science, and the Public](https://kavlicenter.berkeley.edu/) at UC Berkeley.
