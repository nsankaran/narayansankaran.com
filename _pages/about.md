I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Neuroscience at the University of San Francisco. My research seeks a mechanistic account of how the human brain enables music perception - a hallmark of human culture. Such insight informs our understanding of the brain and has the power to drive music-based interventions for treating neurological disorders. To do this work, I combine computational modeling, machine learning, and human neural measurements at different spatial scales using ECoG, MEG, and EEG.
