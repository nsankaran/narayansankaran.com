    title: "Music processing in Disorders of Consciousness"
    excerpt: "In collaboration with the coma lab at UCSF, we examine the neural encoding of music in patients with acute disorders of consciousness (DOC) following traumatic brain injury. DOC exist on a continuum, and accurate differential diagnosis is both essential to clinical management and strongly predicts patient outcomes. However, current bedside examination is imperfect. EEG monitoring of patients has shown that 15-20% of patients diagnosed with coma on bedside examination have evidence of brain responses to motor commands, bringing into question the very definition of consciousness. Harnessing music's power as a modulator of arousal and mood, our long-term goal is to create music-based biomarkers for improved diagnosis and prognosis across impaired states of consciousness."
